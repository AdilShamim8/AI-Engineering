# The Full Roadmap — Sixteen Levels from Writing Software to Shipping AI Systems

Learn AI engineering step by step. Each level builds on the ones before it, and most end with a project you build. **16 levels · 7 phases · 286 sessions · 14 projects.**

> **Source:** this roadmap is adapted verbatim from the [CampusX AI Engineering Roadmap 2026](https://ai-engineer-roadmap-2026.vercel.app/) (retrieved October 17, 2026). Each level page replaces the original's course listings with independently curated free worldwide resources. Short on time? The same material's core ~80% is compressed into the [100-day accelerated roadmap](../accelerated-roadmap/README.md).

## All 16 levels, in order

Go through the phases in order. Each level page contains the full module list with learning objectives, session counts, the level's capstone, its interview and on-the-job weights, and free learning resources.

1. [**Level 0 · Software Engineering Fundamentals**](level-00-software-engineering-fundamentals.md) — become comfortable building, debugging, and deploying real software before adding LLMs. *22 sessions · 8 modules*
2. [**Level 1 · LLM 101**](level-01-llm-101.md) — build a strong mental model of how LLMs work, why they behave the way they do, and how to choose and use the right model for a task. *15 sessions · 12 modules*
3. [**Level 2 · Working with LLM APIs (Orchestration)**](level-02-working-with-llm-apis.md) — turn individual LLM calls into reliable software workflows: messages, streaming, structured outputs, tools, multi-step orchestration, concurrency, failure handling, and observability. *19 sessions · 12 modules*
4. [**Level 3 · Prompt Engineering**](level-03-prompt-engineering.md) — design instructions that make LLM behavior more reliable and predictable. *11 sessions · 9 modules*
5. [**Level 4 · RAG**](level-04-rag.md) — build the retrieval layer that RAG depends on, then ground LLMs in external knowledge and learn the major RAG architectures beyond a naive retrieve-and-generate pipeline. *27 sessions · 15 modules*
6. [**Level 5 · AI Agents**](level-05-ai-agents.md) — build systems where models can decide what to do next, use tools, maintain state, recover from failures, and collaborate with other agents. *22 sessions · 11 modules*
7. [**Level 6 · Context Engineering**](level-06-context-engineering.md) — manage the model's limited context window deliberately, and build the agent harness that applies these decisions across long-running work. *13 sessions · 9 modules*
8. [**Level 7 · LLM Evaluations**](level-07-llm-evaluations.md) — design, execute, and automate comprehensive evaluations for LLM applications to ensure quality, safety, and reliability in production. *17 sessions · 9 modules*
9. [**Level 8 · AI Security**](level-08-ai-security.md) — secure LLM, RAG, and agent systems against model-specific attacks, unsafe actions, data leakage, and regulatory risk. *15 sessions · 9 modules*
10. [**Level 9 · AI System Design**](level-09-ai-system-design.md) — design a complete production AI application and defend every architectural choice across quality, latency, cost, reliability, and user experience. *16 sessions · 10 modules*
11. [**Level 10 · LLMOps: From Development to Production**](level-10-llmops.md) — take the RAG application and the agent you have already built into production — versioned, evaluated on every change, guarded, containerized, deployed on AWS, and monitored. *23 sessions · 11 modules*
12. [**Level 11 · Managed AI Platforms**](level-11-managed-ai-platforms.md) — ship an AI application on the platforms teams actually buy, and know which parts to hand over and which to keep. *18 sessions · 10 modules*
13. [**Level 12 · Multimodal 101: Beyond Text**](level-12-multimodal-101.md) — build a strong mental model of how models handle images, audio, and video, and how to choose the right model when a task stops being text-only. *20 sessions · 13 modules*
14. [**Level 13 · Fine-Tuning**](level-13-fine-tuning.md) — fine-tune a model only when prompting, retrieval, and orchestration have measurably run out, and prove with evaluation numbers that the tune was worth its cost. *16 sessions · 10 modules*
15. [**Level 14 · Projects**](level-14-projects.md) — proof, not concepts. Portfolio builds that compose earlier levels. Ship 3–5 public. *16 sessions · 7 modules*
16. [**Level 15 · GenAI / AI Engineer Interview Prep**](level-15-interview-prep.md) — convert everything learned across the roadmap into interview performance. *16 sessions · 10 modules*

## How to use it

1. **Start where you are.** Each level lists what you need to know first. If you already know it, skip ahead.
2. **Do one level at a time.** Each level has a few modules, and most end with a project. Go in order, because each level builds on the last.
3. **Finish by building.** A level is done when you've built its project and can do everything on its outcome list. Watching the videos is not enough.

Every level page ends with two curated sections: **Free learning resources** (worldwide, free, verified October 2026 — no paid course links) and **In this repository** (the matching curriculum notes from this guide's 101–104 sections).
