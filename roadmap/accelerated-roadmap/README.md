# The Accelerated Roadmap — 100 Days of AI Engineering

Build, evaluate, secure and ship AI apps in **100 days**. This is the job-focused version of the roadmap: it covers the core ~80% of what you need to become an employable AI engineer, and explicitly names what it skips so you know exactly where the full roadmap picks up.

> **Source:** this track is adapted verbatim from the [CampusX 100 Days of AI Engineering](https://ai-engineer-roadmap-2026.vercel.app/100-days/) companion to the AI Engineering Roadmap (retrieved October 17, 2026). Each module page replaces the original's course listings with independently curated free worldwide resources. For the complete 100%-coverage path, see the [full roadmap](../full-roadmap/README.md).

## How to use this track

1. **One day at a time.** Each unit is a day of work; the day ranges are in every module page and unit header.
2. **Do the "Try it" exercises.** Every unit has one — the track is built on building, not watching.
3. **Finish each module's outcome.** Move on when you can do everything in the module's "By the end" statement.
4. **Then defend it.** The interview questions with full model answers and the portfolio projects are the proof that the 100 days worked.
