# Interview Questions

> The AI engineering interview question landscape in 2026 is broader and more specialized than ever. Based on 150+ sources, we've organized questions into six categories.

## The Question Landscape in 2026

AI engineering interviews have diverged significantly from traditional software engineering interviews. While you'll still encounter algorithmic coding questions at some companies, the majority of AI engineering interviews now test a distinct skill set: understanding how LLMs behave, designing systems that are robust to model uncertainty, evaluating AI outputs at scale, and navigating the trade-offs between cost, latency, and quality.

The biggest shifts from 2024 to 2026:

- **Context engineering** has emerged as a first-class topic — candidates are expected to reason about context window management, dynamic context assembly, and multi-source context fusion.
- **Agent design** questions have moved from niche to mainstream — most AI-native companies now test your ability to build, debug, and evaluate autonomous agent systems.
- **Safety and guardrails** are no longer optional — companies like Anthropic, Google DeepMind, and IBM specifically evaluate how you think about PII handling, prompt injection, and content policy.
- **MCP and agent protocols** are the newest addition — the Model Context Protocol and A2A (Agent-to-Agent) protocol are being tested at companies building agent infrastructure.
- **AI-assisted coding** is changing what coding rounds look like — some companies now evaluate your ability to use AI tools effectively, not just your ability to write code from scratch.
