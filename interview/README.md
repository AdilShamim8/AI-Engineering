# Interview preparation: AI engineering

Reviewed October 9, 2026. These materials combine preparation advice, engineering exercises and legacy public interview accounts. They are not a representative survey of company hiring processes.

## Evidence status

The earlier index linked to local job-description and source inventories that are absent from this checkout. Those broken references and unverified source totals have been removed. [The research directory](../research/README.md) now documents a real, pinned job-posting dataset and a reproduced analysis. It does not establish interview-frequency or compensation claims.

Individual references in [the consolidated question collection](questions/questions.md) remain available, but each account needs source-level checking before attribution. An interview anecdote or preparation guide is not evidence that a company currently asks a question or uses a particular process. Use the employer's current instructions for allowed tools, interview format and expectations.

For advanced preparation, use [the competency progression](../learning-paths/advanced-engineering.md). Bring dataset provenance, a reproducible baseline, failed experiments, authorization tests and operational measurements to a project discussion. Do not claim years of experience, benchmark performance or deployment outcomes that you cannot demonstrate.
