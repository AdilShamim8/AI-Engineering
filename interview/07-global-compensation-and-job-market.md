# Compare compensation with evidence

Reviewed October 9, 2026. The earlier “verified global benchmarks” and universal AI salary premium lacked traceable observations, comparable levels and a reproducible method. They have been removed. No current worldwide salary range is asserted here.

## Separate compensation components

| Component | What to record | Common comparison error |
|---|---|---|
| Base salary | Currency, annual period, location, employment type and approved level | Comparing a contractor's revenue with an employee's salary |
| Bonus | Target, guaranteed amount if any, eligibility and payout conditions | Treating a target bonus as guaranteed cash |
| Sign-on | Amount, payment timing and repayment/clawback conditions | Annualizing one-time cash as recurring pay |
| Public-company equity | Grant units, vesting schedule, valuation date and sale restrictions | Treating grant-date value as a guaranteed vest-date value |
| Private-company equity | Instrument, share count, strike price if applicable, vesting, liquidity and dilution terms | Treating a paper valuation or possible tender offer as liquid cash |
| Benefits and taxes | Relevant local benefits, employer costs, tax/residency considerations | Assuming two gross packages have the same net value |

Obtain the actual offer and grant documents. Vesting, liquidity and tax treatment differ by instrument, employer and jurisdiction. A company name or generic “double-trigger RSU” label is not enough to determine the terms. Do not assume a particular company-wide vesting schedule remains current.

## Build a local comparison set

1. Collect current employer-posted bands for the same scope, location and employment type. Record the URL and observation/publication dates. A band is an advertised range, not an observed offer or a market average.
2. Add identifiable survey or offer-report evidence only with its period, sample size, selection method and definition of total compensation. Self-reported platforms can be useful, but their records are not a random sample of all employees.
3. Keep currencies separate. If conversion is useful, document the rate source/date and retain the original amount. Keep Switzerland distinct from EU countries and country-specific APAC markets separate.
4. Report missing disclosures and mismatched levels. Do not infer unstated salaries from job titles, geography, model-generated records or years of experience.
5. Compare recurring cash, one-time cash and equity scenarios separately. A lower/upper advertised band is not a confidence interval or a percentile.

The [job-posting analysis](../research/README.md) does not compute compensation benchmarks. Its counts cannot substantiate a salary premium, equity allocation or callback multiplier.

## Negotiate truthfully and specifically

Ask the recruiter for the approved level, location-specific band and compensation components. Explain the responsibilities you will own and any evidence relevant to level calibration. Discuss base, equity, sign-on, working arrangement and review timing based on the written offer.

Use a competing offer only if you have one. Do not manufacture an offer, a deadline, or willingness to sign immediately. A factual counter-offer can be concise:

```text
Thank you for the offer for [role and location]. I would like to clarify
[level, equity terms, or responsibility scope] before making a decision.

Based on [specific comparable posting/offer and its date, if available]
and the agreed scope of [responsibility], could we discuss [requested change]?

Please confirm the recurring cash, one-time cash, vesting terms, and any
repayment conditions in writing. I can respond by [real decision date].
```

These are decision-making guidelines, not a guarantee of a compensation outcome. Follow the [evidence policy](../research/evidence-policy.md) when publishing salary research.
