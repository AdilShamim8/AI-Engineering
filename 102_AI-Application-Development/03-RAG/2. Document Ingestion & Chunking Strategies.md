## 🤔 STEP 1 — Prediction Before Explanation

> **Let me ask you first:**
> *"Imagine you take a 100-page enterprise financial audit report and chop it into strict blocks of 200 characters each. What happens when a user asks: 'What was the Q3 operating margin for the European cloud division?'"*

A beginner usually thinks:
> *"The vector database will find the chunks with 'operating margin' and 'Q3' and everything will work fine."*

An experienced AI engineer knows:
> **Strict character or naive slicing tears meaning to pieces.**
