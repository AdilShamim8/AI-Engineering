## 🤔 STEP 1 — Prediction Before Explanation

> **Let me ask you first:**
> *"Imagine an enterprise RAG system serving 10,000 corporate employees across Legal, HR, Finance, and Engineering. What happens if an intern asks: 'What were the executive bonuses approved in yesterday's confidential compensation committee meeting?'"*

A beginner usually thinks:
> *"The system will just answer based on the documents stored in the database."*

A production AI engineer knows:
> **Without strict Access Control Lists (ACLs) enforced at the retrieval layer, RAG is a catastrophic data breach waiting to happen.**
> Vector similarity does not care about permissions. If the confidential compensation document is embedded in the same vector collection, vector similarity will faithfully retrieve it and the LLM will happily disclose it to the intern.
>
> **Production RAG requires defense-in-depth: Multi-Tenant RBAC filtering, Semantic Caching, Streaming Responses, Document Lifecycle Invalidation, and Real-Time Observability.**
