## 🤔 STEP 1 — Prediction Before Explanation

> **Let me ask you first:**
> *"Consider two user queries in a corporate enterprise system:*
> *Query A: 'What were the major operational risks identified across all 500 audit reports filed in 2025?'*
> *Query B: 'Compare our European data privacy policy with the one we adopted in California.'*
> *Why will standard vector-similarity RAG fail miserably on both?"*

A beginner usually thinks:
> *"The vector database will just return the most similar chunks and the LLM will summarize them."*

An experienced AI engineer knows:
> **Standard vector RAG is fundamentally local, not global or relational.**
> - For **Query A**: The answer isn't located in any single chunk. It requires holistic entity aggregation across all 500 documents. Vector search will just pull 5 random paragraphs that mention the words "operational risks", completely missing the systemic pattern.
> - For **Query B**: This is a multi-hop comparative query. Standard search collapses both concepts into a single vector midpoint that retrieves muddled chunks from neither policy clearly.
>
> **Advanced RAG introduces Query Transformations, Autonomous Self-Reflective Loops, and Knowledge Graphs (GraphRAG).**
